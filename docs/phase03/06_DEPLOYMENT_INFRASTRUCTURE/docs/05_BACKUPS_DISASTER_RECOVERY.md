# 03.6.5 — Backup, data retention and disaster recovery

RTO 4 hours and RPO 24 hours are **owner-review hypotheses**, not actual guarantees. They depend on selected managed database and asset backup capabilities, data size, restore permissions and measured drills. Daily encrypted snapshots and monthly sampled restore / quarterly full DR exercise are candidate frequencies, not configured schedules. For shorter RPO investigate vendor-supported PITR with verified physical base backup + WAL archive chain; `pg_dump` logical backups do not provide WAL replay PITR.

Backup coverage: DB transactions, auth/session material when safe, content revisions, published snapshots, contact private records, media originals and published asset references, production configuration and separately protected encryption keys. Plan DB/media common recovery point and verify hashes, retention, immutability and isolated backup account. Restrict data scope/geo and deletion to approved privacy policy; restored snapshots may reintroduce erased private/contact records, requiring deletion replay policies and audit.

Drill steps: select known-good recovery point; isolated restore with distinct network/credentials and outbound mail disabled; restore DB+assets, verify migrations, referential integrity, published-only SSR and rejected private preview; reconcile outbox idempotency, revoked sessions and invalidated published URLs; measure actual RTO and RPO, capture evidence; owner authorizes cutover only after validation. Maintain runbooks for PostgreSQL outage and restore, backups, media leak, release rollback. An archive's ZIP integrity is not a PostgreSQL restore test.
